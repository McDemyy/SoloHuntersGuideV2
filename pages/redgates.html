🩸
5% Spawn Rate
Red Gates only appear after a normal gate closes — there's a 5% chance each time. You can't force one to spawn, so keep running normal gates and stay ready.
⚠
Power Warning
Red Gates are significantly harder than their normal versions. Make sure your power exceeds the minimum threshold before entering — or you will struggle.
💡
Same EXP, Better Loot
Red Gates give the same EXP as their normal counterpart but with the chance of exclusive drops not found in regular gates. Always worth entering if you're powerful enough.
