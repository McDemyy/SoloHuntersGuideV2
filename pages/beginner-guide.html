Beginner Guide

Everything you need to start your hunter journey

// How the Game Works

Solo Hunters is a dungeon-crawling RPG where you progress through gates, defeat monsters, and collect loot. Each gate has unique enemies, bosses, and rewards — power, EXP, and gold all increase as you advance. Build your character by equipping armor, weapons, and rings, and enhance them with enchantments. Red Gates are rare supercharged dungeons with extra rewards.

// Enchantment Guide
Fortune
Focus on this if you need the Iron Set — improves drop rates from gates significantly.
Deadly + Sharpshooter
Focus on these once you have Iron Set — boosts damage output and critical hit chance.
// Portals & Gates Explained

Portals are entrances to dungeons, each with unique monsters, bosses, and loot. As you progress, gates become harder and offer better rewards. Red Gates are rare supercharged versions with extra EXP and loot — they spawn with a 5% chance when a normal gate closes. Gate Info shows recommended power, EXP, and gold for each dungeon.

// Tips for Beginners
01Upgrade your gear as soon as possible — better equipment makes progression significantly smoother.
02Try different classes and builds to find your playstyle. Each class has a unique strength profile.
03Don't ignore rings — they provide powerful passive bonuses that stack with armor and weapon stats.
04Check Gate Info before entering a dungeon to make sure your power meets the recommended threshold.
05To get more class rerolls: complete the Zood quest, use Codes, or purchase with Robux.
